# Andrej Karpathy

> TL;DR: AI researcher and educator; a founding member of OpenAI, former Director of AI at Tesla, and author of widely-followed writing on deep learning and LLM workflows. In this wiki, cited as the author of the gist proposing the [[concepts/llm-wiki-pattern]].

## Overview

Karpathy is one of the most-cited practitioners writing about how to actually use LLMs day-to-day, as distinct from how to train them. His public notes — gists, lectures, and essays — regularly set vocabulary for the field (e.g. "vibe coding," "the hallucination problem," and now "LLM Wiki").

## Key facts

- Known for: co-founding OpenAI, leading Tesla Autopilot's computer vision team, his deep learning lecture series ("CS231n," "Zero to Hero"), and a prolific body of public notes on LLM practice.
- Current role (as of the cited source): independent; publishing educational material and writing about LLM workflows.
